# American Renewal Corps — interview pitch

## Objective
Create a polished replacement landing page to present during a startup interview. Keep the result straightforward to hand off and deploy.

## Scope of initial setup
Local Vite + TypeScript foundation with a temporary workspace screen. Final landing-page design, business copy, assets, and production integration are subsequent work.

## Current website review
- Reference: https://americanrenewalcorps.com/
- Reviewed: October 9, 2026.
- Accessible page text shows “Launching Soon,” a contact form, an email-list opt-in, reCAPTCHA notices, and a cookie notice.
- The page does not establish a product offering, audience, traction, or detailed mission. Do not invent those claims in the pitch.

## Design brief for the next phase
Aim for a memorable, editorial landing page with strong typography, responsive layouts, accessible interactions, and deliberate imagery. The current workspace colors and monogram are provisional, not verified company branding.

## Information to establish before final copy
- What the startup does and whom it serves.
- The primary action visitors should take.
- Approved branding, imagery, and any substantiated metrics or testimonials.
- Existing hosting/CMS and the contact-form destination required for replacement.

## Handoff requirements
- Production build in `dist/`.
- No secrets committed to source control.
- Verify desktop/mobile layouts and keyboard navigation once design is implemented.
- Replace the temporary workspace screen and review metadata; remove `noindex` only for an approved production release.
- Connect and verify any contact form before presenting it as operational.
